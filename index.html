01 / PERSONAL PROJECTPYTHON · STREAMLIT · CSS

Study Sprout

A small study habit. A growing companion.

A study task tracker where a virtual plant grows as you finish your list. Four growth stages turn everyday progress into something you can see.

Inside the project +

Create, complete, reopen, delete, and filter tasks. Local JSON storage keeps the task list after a refresh or restart.

Automated tests cover task actions, validation, corrupted data, failed writes, filters, and the completed plant state. A local, single-user application built with Python, Streamlit, and CSS.
